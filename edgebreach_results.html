Key Insights
✓
Best response: DPOs and MSPs
Top simulated CVR was 0.4% for both Data Protection Officers and Security-Focused MSPs (2 conversions each from 1,000 impressions). DPOs converted on the Features grid promise of "Automated security assessments mapped to regulations (e.g., GDPR, ISO 27001, SOC 2)" and "export audit-ready summaries" (27%), while MSPs converted on the multi-tenant outcome. EdgeBreach resonates most when framed as evidence/reporting + operational scale — not as a generic security platform.
!
Common rejection: gated access CTA
"Request access / Request early access / Request partner access" repeatedly created hesitation: 28% of DPO non-converters, 34% of Analyst non-converters, 22% of Small Business IT Manager non-converters, 18% of CIO/CTO non-converters, and 22% of MSP non-converters called out this friction explicitly. The CTA felt like a waitlist or committee step rather than an immediate evaluation path — the single most consistent, fixable blocker to turning interest into action.
↓
All-in-one hero reads too broad
Analysts posted the lowest performance (0.2% simulated CVR; 1 conversion) and directly said the Hero "Find what matters. Prove what's fixed." wasn't specific/differentiated enough (28%). DPOs also flagged the Hero mixing multiple promises ("cloud exposures," "threat detection," "risk management") as making EdgeBreach feel like broad platform positioning (22%). EdgeBreach needs a sharper above-the-fold primary job — "compliance evidence," "risk triage," or "multi-tenant reporting" — rather than leading with everything at once.
↗
Biggest gap: unclear workflow mechanics
Multiple audiences asked for concrete "how it works" details in the visible copy. DPOs wanted clearer workflow steps from inputs → checks → outputs (26%), Analysts wanted the unified "workspace" explained beyond a dashboard (34%), and MSPs wanted what drives SLA-matched scoring/configuration (34%). Bounce rates were not extreme (22.9–26.4%), so people are landing but stalling because the Hero/Screenshot doesn't supply enough operational specificity to commit.
