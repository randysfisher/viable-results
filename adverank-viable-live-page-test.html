What the page is doing well

Reasons the simulated audiences gave for clicking through.

A clear next step carries the page

34% of converting operations managers pointed to "Book a Demo" as a clear next step. The single, unambiguous action is the strongest conversion driver on the page, which is why the doer audience outperforms the two owner audiences.

The vertical callout is landing

28% of converting operations managers cited the explicit "for self-storage operators" positioning. Naming the category up front is doing real work, and it is the piece worth protecting through any rewrite.

What is holding it back

Four issues account for most of the simulated non-conversions across the three audiences.

The demo is an unknown quantity

The top reason people did not convert was uncertainty about what the demo involves: 29% of operations managers and 26% of franchise owners said the page never explains demo length, agenda, or what data or integrations they need to bring. With only "Book a Demo" and "Book Demo Now" repeated, busy buyers cannot pre-qualify the time or effort, so they defer even when interested.

Two products create one extra decision

21% of franchise owners and 18% of independent owners were held up by "Two Products. One Marketing System." combined with the Cloud and Blocks naming. They are not sure which one they are buying first. That ambiguity adds a decision ahead of the demo click, and it costs you the buyer who has one urgent problem, ads or website, and wants a single starting path.

Proof sits too far from the pitch

22% of independent owners held back because the proof near the core pitch is thin. Case studies appear as headlines such as "14.7x Return" without scannable on-page context to support them, so the number reads as a claim rather than evidence.

Integration promises go unnamed

19% of franchise owners cited missing implementation specifics. The page promises "Connected to your management software" and to follow every click through to a signed lease, but it never names the systems or explains how the tracking works. Trust stays conditional, and conditional trust slows the demo click.

Recommended next steps

Both actions are aimed at converting the demand the page is already generating, rather than finding new demand.

Add demo and rollout specifics

This is a messaging gap, not a demand gap. The best audience still hit a 3.2% simulated conversion rate, but 29% of them did not book because the demo and the setup are never explained. Closing that gap costs three lines of copy.

Next step Add three short lines beside "Book a Demo" stating demo length, agenda, and what the buyer needs to bring. For example: 20 minutes, we review Google Ads and occupancy, confirm your management software, and show budget recommendations.

Focus outreach on franchise owners

Franchise owners scored highest on message-audience fit at 6.7 out of 10, a measure of how closely the page matches what that audience cares about, and still reached a 2.8% simulated conversion rate. The fit is there ahead of the traffic.

Next step Tailor the top of the page to the multi-location language already resonating, since 27% cited the ads to website to signed lease story, then drive franchise and operator group traffic to that same page.

How to read this report

What was tested

The live Adverank landing page, unchanged, run through a simulated conversion funnel against three self-storage buyer audiences at 1,000 views each.

What the numbers mean

Simulated CVR is CTA clicks divided by landing page views. Message-audience fit scores how closely the page matches what an audience cares about, on a 10 point scale.

What it is good for

Directional evidence about which message and audience to back next. It is not a forecast of live conversion rate. Real-person testing is the layer that produces proof.
