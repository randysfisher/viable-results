The opening

Monitoring tools see change but can't tell you if it matters. Research tools can test, but only when someone asks.

Viable can do both. When a competitor rewrites its hero or your live page changes, Viable can run the new version against the same synthetic audience and show the conversion delta. Crayon can't do that, and Synthetic Users doesn't watch for changes. Viable already has the ingredients: Recommendations already use test history, competitive intelligence and industry trends.

Part 2 · Feature candidates

Nine features, in three build tiers

The numbers match the pins on the wireframe below. Tier 1 creates the habit of coming back. Tier 2 makes the value compound. Tier 3 fits the agency beachhead.

Tier 1 · Build first

The reason to open the app on a Monday
1

What Changed feed

A short AI summary, then the three most important insights since the last visit: one competitor change, one change on your own page, and one audience shift.

FromAmplitude Dashboard Agent, Contentsquare Sense
2

Competitor Watch

Track 3 to 5 competitor URLs. When their messaging or pricing changes, Viable automatically simulates the new page against your audience and shows their CVR next to yours, with a one-click head-to-head test.

FromCrayon/Klue, plus a simulation layer only Viable has
3

Recommended tests

Surface the top three from the Recommendations tab inside What Changed, one from each source: competitor analysis, a prior test, and industry and market trends. Each explains why it's suggested and opens a pre-filled test.

FromContentsquare next-best-action, your existing Recommendations engine

Tier 2 · Build next

Value that compounds with every test
4

Live Page Monitor

Re-run live page tests weekly or after a site change. Flag regressions, for example "PM page fell from 3.2% to 2.4% after the Sep 26 update."

FromAmplitude anomaly monitoring, Dovetail early-warning alerts
5

Viability Pulse

A full-width performance section: overall, best and lowest CVR, tests run, and viability mix. Adds a CVR chart plotted against the tier bands and a peer benchmark, coverage by test type and audience, and a best-vs-lowest comparison that explains the difference.

FromDovetail longitudinal dashboards, Wynter benchmarks
6

Audience Pulse

Saved audiences get re-surveyed on a light cadence. Show which objections and motivations are rising, for example "AI-trust objection up 18% among PMs."

FromWynter brand tracking, Dovetail theme trends

Tier 3 · Scale

Built for agencies and teams
7

Result freshness + decision log

Every test shows its age and whether the market has moved since (for example, two competitors changed). Each test gets a decision state: pending, shipped, or killed.

FromAmplitude "consistent vs. different" framing
8

Client portfolio view

For agencies, a workspace-level view shows the health of every client's concepts, which clients need a re-test, and which briefs are ready to forward.

FromYour GTM: agencies are the beachhead
9

Digest delivery

Send the What Changed summary and top signals to Slack or email on a schedule, with a link back. The dashboard comes to the user.

FromAmplitude, Dovetail, Klue newsletters
