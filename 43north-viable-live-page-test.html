What the page is doing well

Signals worth protecting through any rewrite.

The founders section works when people reach it

34% of local converters pointed to the "FOR FOUNDERS" framing around funding and an immersive accelerator program. That audience also posted a 5.4% landing page conversion rate once on the page. The section is not the problem. Its position is.

The money proof is credible

Two specific claims did real work: 26% cited "$5 million annually since 2014" and 22% cited "five companies secure $1M each." Concrete, verifiable numbers make the offer feel real and valuable, which is exactly the treatment the rest of the program mechanics are missing.

What is holding it back

Four issues account for most of the simulated non-conversions, and the first one explains most of the gap between audiences.

The page opens like an events hub

Across three audiences, 34% cited the same blocker: above-the-fold content is dominated by event and community messaging such as "The Final Finals" and "Pitch Your Buffalo" before the founders pathway appears. That ordering tracks directly with the three weak results, at 1.7%, 2.1%, and 1.8%. Founders who do not scroll assume they are on a news and events hub rather than an application path.

"Learn More" is not an application

Call to action wording is a drag on every audience. 25% of seed-stage, 24% of local mentorship, 24% of clean tech, and 22% of spin-off visitors rejected the page because the primary founder action is "Learn More" rather than "Apply" or "Check eligibility." Even people who like the $1M hook treat the page as browsing, so fewer take a committed step.

No audience cleared 5 out of 10 on fit

Message-audience fit stayed weak in every pipeline, from 4.3 out of 10 for university spin-offs to 4.9 out of 10 for local mentorship and clean tech. Between 57% and 62% of simulated users fell into the weak similarity bucket. This is a page-wide ceiling, not one audience underperforming.

Founders cannot self-qualify

The most repeated reason behind those fit scores is missing program mechanics: eligibility, timeline, and selection steps, cited by 20% to 26% of users depending on audience. Without them, a founder cannot tell whether the program is for them, so the decision gets deferred rather than made.

Recommended next steps

The first action addresses the gap between audiences. The second works around it while the page is being changed.

Put the application path above the events

This is a messaging and flow problem. Your best segment still only reached a 4.2% simulated conversion rate, and 28% to 34% across audiences said the page opens with "Final Finals" and "Pitch Your Buffalo" instead of the accelerator path.

Next step Reorder the top section so "FOR FOUNDERS, Scale your business" appears first, and change the founder call to action from "Learn More" to "Apply" or "Check eligibility." Put three bullets right beside it: stage, meaning late pre-seed and seed, timeline and cohort dates, and selection steps.

Focus outreach on mentorship-led founders

Local and clean tech audiences tied for the highest message-audience fit at 4.9 out of 10, a measure of how closely the copy matches what that audience cares about. Local mentorship seekers also delivered the highest simulated conversion rate at 4.2%, or 42 actions per 1,000 impressions.

Next step Aim near-term distribution at local founder channels, and point that traffic directly at the "FOR FOUNDERS" section rather than the top of the page, until the ordering is fixed.

How to read this report

What was tested

The live 43North Startup Accelerator Program landing page, unchanged, run through a simulated conversion funnel against four founder audiences at 1,000 views each on September 1, 2026.

What the numbers mean

Simulated CVR is CTA clicks divided by landing page views. Message-audience fit scores how closely the page matches what an audience cares about, on a 10 point scale. Reason percentages are per audience as reported.

What it is good for

Directional evidence about which message and audience to back next. It is not a forecast of live conversion rate. Real-person testing is the layer that produces proof.
