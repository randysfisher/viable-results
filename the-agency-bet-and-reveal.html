WHAT RESONATED

The strongest product-audience fit is on “lighter sipping without sacrificing flavor."

WHAT CREATED FRICTION

Social Young Adults and Outdoor Entertainers each scored 7.3/10 on message-audience fit (with 40% strong matches), yet only reached 0.3% and 0.2% simulated conversion rates. In contrast, Health-Conscious Wine Drinkers scored just 5.6/10 (60% weak matches) but still produced the highest conversion rate at 1.1%. This suggests the core promise (low-calorie, low-ABV citrus spritz) can sell, but the creative targeting/messaging match needs tightening to lift results without changing the product.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

Sharpen the CTA and add visible proof, then re-run to confirm the lift before committing budget to build.

Why High Viability? 1.1% of ad impressions turned into CTA actions in the Health-Conscious Wine Drinkers pipeline (11 actions from 1,000 impressions), beating the other two audiences at 0.3% and 0.2%. That pipeline also combined 7.9% ad click-through with a 13.9% landing-page conversion rate, driven by the Hero’s “only 110 calories” and “6.8% ABV” promise plus concrete citrus mechanism (“real grapefruit + lemon extracts”).
