Key Insights
✓
Budget and beginner audiences convert best
Budget-Conscious Anglers and Newcomers to Fishing both hit 0.4% simulated CVR, beating Gear Enthusiasts at 0.3%. Top conversion drivers: "without paying retail" (34%) and "get started fishing without overspending" (31%). Recast resonates most when leading with savings and accessibility — not collector identity.
!
Trust and safety stay too vague
The biggest repeated gap is missing concrete trust mechanics. Collectors wanted clearer "trusted marketplace" specifics (41%), and newcomers wanted what makes transactions "safe or reliable" beyond "Easy buy/sell flow" (41%). Recast loses conversions because the Hero promises trust, but the page doesn't say what actually enforces it.
↓
Transaction flow clarity blocks clicks
Newcomers cited hesitation because "closing the deal/final handoff" language stayed vague (19%), with a dedicated flow clarity gap at 33%. CTR is strong (3–5.6%) but bounce is low — uncertainty after landing. Recast needs a clearer step-by-step "how it works" explanation in the Hero or Features section.
↗
Hero works — CTAs feel shallow
The Hero nailed intent ("worth keeping" + "For collectors & gear needs") at 34%, yet 33% didn't convert because Browse listings / Browse Recast Market didn't add detail for fast evaluators. Budget users also hesitated when the message split between buying and selling (21%), making the next step feel less focused.
Recommended Next Actions
Real Person Test
Run a demand test with top-fit US users
Prioritize audiences that best understood the pitch. Fishing Gear Enthusiasts scored 6.7/10 on message-audience fit; Budget-Conscious Anglers scored 6.6/10. Run a real-person test with these two groups using the current landing page and track whether adding concrete trust and flow proof changes sign-ups.

Make trust and deal flow explicit
Optimize the best-performing variant (0.4% CVR) by rewriting the Hero subheadline + one Features row to spell out what "trusted" and "easy buy/sell flow" actually mean. 38% flagged vague trust language and 29% wanted clearer "search/compare" details. Rerun the same audiences with updated above-the-fold copy and a single focused primary CTA — either buy or sell — to reduce the 21% split-focus hesitation.
